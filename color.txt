# KR ENVIRON SOLUTIONS
## Official Website Color Theme & Design System

**Version:** 1.0  
**Purpose:** Website design, UI/UX, social media marketing, brochures, presentations, and brand consistency.

---

## 1. BRAND COLOR PALETTE

### Primary Color — Forest Green
**HEX:** #216B35

**Purpose:** Main brand color, primary buttons, important icons, key links, and section headings.

**Brand meaning:** Nature, environmental responsibility, growth, trust, and sustainability.

### Secondary Color — Environmental Blue
**HEX:** #087EA4

**Purpose:** Secondary buttons, technical sections, links, GIS and remote sensing graphics, and environmental monitoring content.

**Brand meaning:** Water, science, technology, research, and reliability.

### Accent Color — Solar Gold
**HEX:** #F2BE36

**Purpose:** Small highlights, special icons, important figures, badges, and decorative elements.

**Brand meaning:** Solar energy, innovation, optimism, and sustainability.

**Text rule:** Use dark text on gold backgrounds rather than white text for better readability.

**Note:** These are recommended digital brand colors inspired by the supplied KR Environ Solutions logo. They have not been verified as exact color values from the original logo file.

## 3. TYPOGRAPHY COLOR RULES

### H1 — Main Page Heading
- Color: #173B26
- Suggested desktop font size: 48–56 px
- Font weight: 700
- Usage: Homepage hero title and main page heading.
- Example: Environmental Solutions for a Sustainable Future

### H2 — Section Headings
- Color: #216B35
- Suggested desktop font size: 32–40 px
- Font weight: 600–700
- Usage: About Us, Our Services, Our Expertise, Why Choose Us, and Our Projects.

### H3 — Subheadings
- Color: #26382D
- Suggested font size: 22–26 px
- Font weight: 600
- Usage: Individual services, service cards, technical features, and subsection titles.

### Paragraph Text
- Color: #4B5563
- Suggested font size: 16–18 px
- Font weight: 400
- Line height: 1.6–1.8
- Usage: Descriptions, company introduction, service explanations, and website body content.

### Secondary Text
- Color: #68756D
- Suggested font size: 14–16 px
- Usage: Captions, supporting information, labels, and secondary descriptions.

### Links
- Default color: #087EA4
- Hover color: #06617F
- Underline: Recommended for links within paragraph text.

**Typography rule:** Keep headings dark and prominent, paragraph text neutral, and accent colors limited to selected details.

## 5. WEBSITE BACKGROUND RULES

### White — #FFFFFF
Use for the main website background, header, navigation, hero content areas, service cards, forms, and enquiry sections.

### Soft Green-White — #F5F8F3
Use for About Us, Why Choose KR Environ, team and expertise sections, sustainability content, and alternating content sections.

### Pale Blue — #EFF7FA
Use for GIS and remote sensing, environmental monitoring, technical capabilities, research, and scientific assessment sections.

### Deep Forest — #142D20
Use for the footer, selected dark banners, and occasional high-impact sections.

For dark backgrounds, use white headings (#FFFFFF) and soft light text (#E4EEE6).

---

## 6. RECOMMENDED COLOR PROPORTIONS

Use the following visual balance as a starting point:

- **70% — Neutral backgrounds:** White and very light backgrounds.
- **20% — Forest green:** Branding, headings, primary buttons, and key icons.
- **8% — Environmental blue:** Technical graphics, links, and secondary buttons.
- **2% — Solar gold:** Small highlights and decorative elements.

These proportions are practical design guidelines, not strict requirements.

---

## 7. BRAND DESIGN RULES

1. Use forest green as the main brand color across the entire website.
2. Use environmental blue as the supporting technical color.
3. Use solar gold sparingly.
4. Maintain white space and a clean, uncluttered interface.
5. Alternate white, soft green-white, and pale blue backgrounds to separate sections.
6. Maintain the same color palette across every page and component.
7. Use consistent typography, spacing, buttons, icons, and card styles.
8. Avoid excessive gradients, strong shadows, neon colors, and too many competing accent colors.
9. Use authentic environmental, forest, wildlife, fieldwork, and GIS imagery wherever appropriate.
10. Keep the website professional, scientific, credible, and suitable for institutional and business audiences.

---

## 8. ACCESSIBILITY RULES

- Use white text on forest-green and environmental-blue buttons.
- Avoid white text on solar gold.
- Keep paragraph text dark enough to read comfortably against light backgrounds.
- Check text and interactive-element contrast before publishing.
- Use visible keyboard-focus states and clear hover states.
- Do not communicate important information through color alone.
