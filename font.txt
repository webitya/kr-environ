2. BRAND IDENTITY & TYPOGRAPHY THEORY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
KR Environ Solution operates at the intersection of environmental stewardship, 
cutting-edge engineering, ecological sustainability, and industrial compliance.

The selected font ecosystem provides a multi-tiered communication palette:
- Urgency & Environmental Consciousness (Climate Crisis)
- Corporate Authority & Visionary Leadership (Montserrat)
- Modern Engineering & Precision Analytics (Manrope)
- Engaging Interactivity & Approachability (Poppins)
- Editorial Trust & Warmth (Lato)
- Technical Clarity & Compliance Data (Open Sans)


3. FONT ROLES & DESIGN HIERARCHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[A] "Climate Crisis"
    * Classification: Experimental Environmental Display / Variable Font
    * CSS Family: font-family: 'Climate Crisis', sans-serif;
    * Brand Meaning: Designed to visually embody Arctic ice-loss data; symbolizes 
      ecological gravity, urgency, and transformative environmental impact.
    * Target Usage:
      - High-impact Hero accent words (e.g., "NET ZERO", "CLIMATE", "CRISIS TO SOLUTION")
      - Key Environmental Milestone banners & urgency callouts
      - Large display percentage/emission reduction badges
    * Rule: Use sparingly in uppercase for short, punchy statements. Avoid for full sentences.

[B] "Montserrat"
    * Classification: Geometric Sans-Serif
    * CSS Family: font-family: 'Montserrat', sans-serif;
    * Recommended Weights: 600 (SemiBold), 700 (Bold), 800 (ExtraBold)
    * Brand Meaning: Architectural, structural, and dependable. Projects corporate 
      reputation, industry stature, and forward-thinking sustainability leadership.
    * Target Usage:
      - Main Page Titles (H1)
      - Major Section Headings (H2)
      - Main Navigation Bar brand logotype & primary nav items
      - Value Proposition headlines

[C] "Manrope"
    * Classification: Modern Semi-Geometric Sans-Serif
    * CSS Family: font-family: 'Manrope', sans-serif;
    * Recommended Weights: 400 (Regular), 500 (Medium), 600 (SemiBold), 700 (Bold)
    * Brand Meaning: Crisp, technological, and data-driven. Connects environmental 
      mission with engineering precision and modern digital execution.
    * Target Usage:
      - Subheadings (H3, H4)
      - KPI Metrics, Statistics, and Data Dashboards (e.g., "99.8% Emission Reduction")
      - Service Card titles & Solution Feature highlights
      - Process timeline milestones

[D] "Poppins"
    * Classification: Rounded Geometric Sans-Serif
    * CSS Family: font-family: 'Poppins', sans-serif;
    * Recommended Weights: 500 (Medium), 600 (SemiBold), 700 (Bold)
    * Brand Meaning: Human-centric, welcoming, action-oriented. Drives engagement 
      and makes technical eco-solutions approachable to clients and partners.
    * Target Usage:
      - Call to Action (CTA) buttons (e.g., "Request an Environmental Audit", "Get in Touch")
      - Interactive badges, tags, filter chips (e.g., "Air Quality", "EIA", "Waste Water")
      - Form labels and input fields
      - Testimonial author titles & micro-copy

[E] "Lato"
    * Classification: Humanist Sans-Serif
    * CSS Family: font-family: 'Lato', sans-serif;
    * Recommended Weights: 300 (Light), 400 (Regular), 400i (Italic), 700 (Bold)
    * Brand Meaning: Transparent, warm, organic, and grounded. Enhances readability 
      and establishes long-form storytelling credibility.
    * Target Usage:
      - Primary Body Paragraphs across all marketing and service pages
      - Detailed project case studies and sustainability whitepapers
      - "About KR Environ" company story and mission statements
      - Client testimonial quotes

[F] "Open Sans"
    * Classification: Neutral Neutral-Humanist Sans-Serif
    * CSS Family: font-family: 'Open Sans', sans-serif;
    * Recommended Weights: 400 (Regular), 600 (SemiBold)
    * Brand Meaning: Neutral, universal legibility, regulatory transparency.
    * Target Usage:
      - Technical specification tables & emission parameter matrices
      - Compliance certifications, ISO standards, and legal disclosures
      - Footer site map, policy notes, and contact meta details
      - Tooltips and secondary fine print
